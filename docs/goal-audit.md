(verification completed 2026-09-08 UTC). This checklist translates the
[implementation goal](../IMPLEMENTATION_GOAL.md) and
[interoperability cases](../use-cases/interoperability.md) into reviewable work.
Document/tool deliverables and the corrected runtime evidence below have been
reviewed. Audit IDs below are
tracking identifiers, not substitutes for the draft's normative requirements.

Work items use `in progress` or `demonstrated`; unexecuted integration tests use
`not run`. Later readiness gates remain `unresolved`, separately identifying
their completed present-stage plan or scope.
Demonstrated requires linked evidence; a document's existence proves only its
document deliverable. Test reports distinguish `passed`, `failed`, `unsupported`
and `not run`. Unsupported or unrun mandatory core tests do not pass. Record
later-gate scope separately from status; unavailable CAD does not waive core
MCP, preservation, binding or failure behavior.

Evidence entries should identify artifacts, exact commands, implementation and
dependency revisions, suite/profile versions, environment, expected and observed
results, and limitations. Link retained reports rather than relying on console
summaries. Every required normative rule needs a requirement-to-test mapping.
